## SRB

U okviru predmeta RF Elektronika rađen je projekat koji je predstavljen u ovom direktorijumu. Na kursu stečena je teoretska osnova kola na RF učestanostima i osnova u dizajniranju kola u 65nm CMOS integrisanoj tehnologiji.

Za projekat napravljen je - duplo balansirani mešač sa potiskivanjem slike - koji usko prati rad "A 28GHz Quadrature Up-conversion Transmitter in 65nm CMOS for 5G mmWave Radio" koji je takođe dat u ovom direktorijumu. Nadam se da me autori neće tužiti za autorska prava. 

Fajlovi izrađeni u Cadance Virtuoso softverskom alatu su dati u direktorijumu `Cadance`.

## ENG

As part of the RF Electronics course, a project was completed and is presented in this directory. The course provided a theoretical foundation in RF-frequency circuits, as well as the fundamentals of circuit design using 65 nm CMOS integrated technology.

For the project, a double-balanced image-reject mixer was designed, closely following the paper “A 28GHz Quadrature Up-conversion Transmitter in 65nm CMOS for 5G mmWave Radio,” which is also included in this directory. Hopefully, the authors won't sue me for copyright infringement.

The files created using the Cadence Virtuoso software are provided in the `Cadance` directory.
